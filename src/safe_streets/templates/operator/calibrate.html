1. The scene

Draw on the snapshot, then Save the scene. Road: the corners of the road surface you can see clearly (where motion is watched). Lane: the part of the road whose cars count, if not all of it (say, the near lane). Mask: areas whose motion to ignore (a flag, a tree in the wind); each New mask starts another. Night lane: two clicks along the line between the lanes, for cars seen only by their lights. Occluder: click a post or tree trunk that hides cars for a moment. Saved before any drive-bys, the scene is a draft: passes are tracked and kept but get no speed until step 2.
