The Day Dial

slowfast

A 24-hour clock, midnight at the top. The hand sweeps the day; each vehicle grows out as a line when the hand reaches its time, its length and heat its speed, and every speeder sends out a pulse as it crosses the dashed ring, the limit. The outer arc thickens with each hour's traffic. Afterwards the day keeps breathing, replaying a car now and then. Hover or tap a line to read it; drag the slider to scrub.
